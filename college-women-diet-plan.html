College women daily eating plan

A simple, affordable starting plan for study days, hostel life and regular training.

Morning

Oats with milk or curd, one banana and 6 almonds. Add 2 eggs or paneer/tofu for protein.

College time

2 rotis or rice, dal/chana, paneer/eggs/chicken and one bowl of vegetables. Carry fruit or roasted chana.

Evening

Sprouts chaat, curd or a banana with peanut butter. Drink water before tea or coffee.

Night

2 rotis or a small rice serving with dal and paneer/tofu/eggs, plus salad or cooked vegetables.

Adjust portions to hunger, activity and health needs. This is an educational starting point, not a medical prescription. Consult a qualified dietitian for a personalised plan.
